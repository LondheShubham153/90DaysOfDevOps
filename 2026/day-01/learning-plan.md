# 90 Days DevOps Learning Plan

## Current Level
I am a **college student / fresher** with a Computer Science background.  
I have basic knowledge of Linux, programming fundamentals, and how software works, but I am new to real-world DevOps practices and production systems.

## My Understanding of DevOps & Cloud
DevOps is not just tools; it is a culture of **ownership, automation, reliability, and continuous improvement**.  
Cloud Engineering enables scalable, reliable infrastructure where applications can be built, deployed, and monitored efficiently.  
A DevOps engineer bridges development and operations by ensuring fast, stable, and repeatable delivery.

## Why I Am Learning DevOps & Cloud
I am learning DevOps to:
- Build **real-world, production-ready skills**
- Understand how modern systems work end-to-end
- Become a reliable engineer who can own systems, not just write code
- Create strong career opportunities in cloud and platform engineering

I want skills that compound over time, not just certificates.

## Where I Want to Reach in 90 Days
By the end of 90 days, I want to be capable of working with real systems confidently.

### My 3 Clear Goals
1. Deploy a **production-grade application** using Docker and Kubernetes
2. Build a **complete CI/CD pipeline** with automated testing and deployment
3. Gain confidence in **Linux, cloud basics, and debugging failures**

## Core DevOps Skills I Will Build
1. **Linux & System Fundamentals** – processes, networking, logs, troubleshooting  
2. **CI/CD & Automation** – Git, pipelines, build → test → deploy workflows  
3. **Containers & Kubernetes** – Docker, Kubernetes basics, deployment and debugging  

## Time Commitment & Consistency Plan
- **Weekdays:** 2 to 2.5 hours per day  
- **Weekends:** 4 to 6 hours per day  
- Total focus: consistency over perfection

### How I Will Stay Consistent
- Study daily, even on low-motivation days
- Learn in public by sharing progress and notes
- Focus on finishing tasks, not over-planning
- Treat this like a job, not a hobby

## Commitment to Myself
For the next 90 days, I commit to:
- Showing up daily
- Taking ownership of my learning
- Building discipline before chasing motivation

This is not an experiment.
This is my execution phase.
